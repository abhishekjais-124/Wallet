Wallet privacy policy

Wallet is a personal app for keeping payment cards and identity documents. It is used by its owner only.

What is stored

Everything you add is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. The server that delivers the app never receives your cards, documents, PIN or recovery key.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, Wallet asks only for the drive.appdata permission. It stores the encrypted vault and encrypted attachments in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive and cannot see any other file. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Removing your data

Erase the wallet in Settings, sign out of Google in the app, and remove Wallet's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Its hidden Drive data can be deleted in Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.
